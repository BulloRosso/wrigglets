# Company overview

Sofaworks makes three sofa models: **Lund** (2-seater), **Oslo** (3-seater) and **Bergen** (corner sofa).

## Factories

- **Werk Nord** (F-NORTH, Hamburg) and **Werk Süd** (F-SOUTH, Stuttgart).
- Each factory has three machines in series (frame cutter, upholstery line, assembly line) and two workers.
- A machine that is down stops the whole factory: the three machines work in series.

## Conventions

- Production orders have a priority from 1 (most urgent) to 9. **5 is the default**; new work gets 6 or higher unless it is
  due earlier, so that it does not push existing orders late.
- A sofa reaches the customer **three calendar days** after the end of production.
- The measure of success of every measure is the KPI in the ERP (`erp__get_kpi`): late orders, late sofas, unplanned
  orders, material shortages. Look at all late orders, not only at the ones you touched.
