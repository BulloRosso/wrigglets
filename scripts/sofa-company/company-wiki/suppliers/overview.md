# Suppliers

Prices, lead times and order limits change: read them with `erp__list_suppliers`. What holds over time:

- **Pine beams:** Nordholz Sägewerk is the main supplier. Baltic Timber (Tallinn) and Silesia Wood are the alternatives;
  each has a maximum quantity per order, so a large need is split over several orders or suppliers.
- **Fabric:** Tuchwerk Bielefeld is the main supplier for both fabrics and offers express delivery. Tessile Como supplies
  Oatmeal as an alternative.
- **Spindle motor for the frame cutters:** Maschinenbau Reiter is the **only** supplier of `SP-SPINDLE-220`. A repair
  cannot start before the part arrives, so order it immediately; express shortens the repair by several days.
- A supplier that reports that it cannot deliver refuses new orders until it is able to deliver again.
